# Supply Chain Risk & Demand Intelligence

An advanced analytics project designed to analyze supply chain operations, forecast product demand, evaluate supplier risk, optimize inventory, and monitor delivery performance.

This project combines **Python, Java, SQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and data analytics** to create a multi-layer supply chain intelligence system.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Modern supply chains generate large amounts of operational data involving suppliers, products, warehouses, orders, transportation, inventory, and customer demand.

The objective of this project is to transform this operational data into actionable business intelligence.

The system focuses on:

- Demand forecasting
- Inventory optimization
- Supplier risk assessment
- Procurement analytics
- Delivery performance
- Warehouse performance
- Stock-out detection
- Overstock identification
- Order analysis
- Supply chain risk scoring
- Business intelligence dashboards

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The major objectives of the project are:

1. Analyze historical supply chain transactions.
2. Identify demand patterns across products and regions.
3. Forecast future product demand.
4. Identify high-risk suppliers.
5. Analyze supplier delivery performance.
6. Monitor inventory levels.
7. Detect potential stock-out situations.
8. Identify excess inventory.
9. Analyze procurement spending.
10. Evaluate logistics and delivery performance.
11. Build supply chain risk indicators.
12. Generate management-level business insights.
